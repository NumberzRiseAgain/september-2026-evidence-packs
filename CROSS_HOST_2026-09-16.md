## What the day established

Four classes of reproduction, observed on one machine in seven minutes: bit-identical (DEBRIEFLINE, RIGLINE, TORQUELINE, SPEEDDIAL, ARRESTLINE, and three of SABER's seven experiments), last-bit floating point (SABER e2, e4, e4b), Monte-Carlo floor (APERTURELINE, 0.0026 against a 0.005 tolerance), and stack-determined (SIGNALCAST's learned stage, which reproduces the recorded run exactly on the same stack and moves by up to 0.06 on another). Timing fields move everywhere and should never be quoted from a single host.

The run identifier tracks the source tree, so a paper-side script added under `08_Analysis/code/` moves the id without touching a result. Two ways to keep an id stable once a volume quotes it: keep paper tooling out of the manifest's source set, or refresh the quoted id at rebuild.

Eight packs now carry a founder's row in `RUN_RECORD.md`; CREWLINE, DECKLINE and SISR get theirs after the three installs above (done 19 Sep, next section).
